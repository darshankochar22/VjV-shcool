1. WHAT IS VJV?

Vichakshan Jain Vidyapeeth is a CBSE-affiliated, co-education day-cum-residential school for Nursery to Class XII, set on a 14-acre campus in Kumhari, Chhattisgarh.

2. WHY VJV?

Shiksha, Sewa and Sadhana — learning, service and self-discipline, guided by Jain values, with hostels for boys and girls.
